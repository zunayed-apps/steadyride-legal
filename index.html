Privacy Policy

SteadyRide (com.day2day.steadyride) · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Summary: SteadyRide does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party trackers.

Who we are

SteadyRide is developed by MD Zunayed Islam Pranto (day2day.apps). You can reach us any time at zunayed.apps@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. SteadyRide does not collect or transmit any personal or device information. We do not use analytics, advertising, or any third‑party SDKs that gather data.

How the app uses device features (all on‑device)

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, etc.): read in real time to move the on‑screen cues with your vehicle. This data stays on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Location (optional): used only if you enable Automatic mode, to detect from speed that you are travelling so the cues can start on their own. Location is processed on your device in real time, is never stored, and is never transmitted off your device. Automatic mode is optional; the app works fully without location.
  • Display over other apps: lets the motion cues appear on top of other apps. It does not read or capture the content of any other app.
  • Notifications: used only to show the ongoing notice Android requires while the cue service is running.
  • Internet: used only to open web pages you choose to view (such as this policy) and Google Play pages. The app does not send any data over the internet.

Your settings

Your preferences (for example cue style, theme, language, and on/off mode) are saved only on your device so the app remembers them. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

Google Play services

SteadyRide is distributed through Google Play. The purchase of the app, the optional in‑app rating prompt, and Google Play's check that the app was installed from Google Play are handled by Google, not by us. We do not receive your payment details or any personal information from these features. Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Data retention and deletion

We do not retain any data because we do not collect any. Settings stored on your device are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

SteadyRide is a general‑purpose utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no personal data for us to secure on a server. Any processing happens locally on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
